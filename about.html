Ch. 03 — The minister

A life set aside
for the ministry of song.

Deborah Ufedejo is a Christian music minister whose calling is to draw hearts closer to Jesus through worship — one song, one gathering, one surrendered moment at a time.

Biography

A ministry of presence.

Deborah's ministry is centred on one desire — that people would encounter Jesus. Whether in an intimate worship setting, a Sunday ministration, or a night of worship before hundreds, the aim never shifts.

She sings not to be heard, but so that He is heard. Her calling is to hold space — for surrender, for stillness, for the tender work of the Holy Spirit — and to lead others into that same posture through the ministry of song.

Full biographical details, discography and ministry history will be published here as the ministry grows.

Ch. 04 — What we hold

The heart behind
the ministry.

Mission

To glorify God through worship and ministry, creating Christ-centered experiences that draw people closer to Jesus, awaken hearts to His presence, and inspire transformed lives through the power of the Gospel.

Vision

To raise a generation that encounters Jesus deeply, grows in their relationship with Him, and carries His presence into their families, communities, churches, and nations through worship and a life surrendered to God.
