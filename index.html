Ch. 03 — The minister

Meet Deborah.

Deborah Ufedejo is a Christian music minister devoted to leading people into the presence of Jesus through worship and song.

Her ministry moves quietly and intentionally — through churches, conferences, worship nights and gatherings where people are hungry for God to move. Wherever she is invited, the goal is always the same: that Jesus would be seen, heard and encountered.

Ch. 05 — Why we exist

To make Jesus known — through worship, through song, through a life surrendered.

Mission

To glorify God through worship and ministry, creating Christ-centered experiences that draw people closer to Jesus, awaken hearts to His presence, and inspire transformed lives through the power of the Gospel.

Vision

To raise a generation that encounters Jesus deeply, grows in their relationship with Him, and carries His presence into their families, communities, churches, and nations through worship and a life surrendered to God.
